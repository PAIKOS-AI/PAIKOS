# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and respectful community for all contributors.

---

## Expected Behavior

✅ **Be respectful** — Treat everyone with kindness  
✅ **Be inclusive** — Welcome all backgrounds and perspectives  
✅ **Be collaborative** — Work together constructively  
✅ **Be professional** — Keep interactions focused and appropriate  
✅ **Be honest** — Communicate truthfully

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

❌ Harassment or discriminatory comments  
❌ Bullying, threats, or intimidation  
❌ Sexual or inappropriate content  
❌ Spam or trolling  
❌ Doxxing or sharing personal information

---

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior:

1. Report to **conduct@paikos.app** with details
2. Include what happened, when, and who was involved
3. Keep reports confidential

We'll respond within 5 days and take appropriate action.

---

**By participating, you agree to uphold this Code of Conduct.**
